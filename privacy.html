Privacy Policy - The Multitrack Player

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how The Multitrack Player ("we", "our" or "the application") collects, uses, and protects the information of users ("you" or "user") of our mobile application for iOS (iPhone and iPad) and Android, including its CarPlay and Android Auto experiences. Unless a section says otherwise, it applies equally to both platforms.

By using The Multitrack Player, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

Signing in is optional: you can use the application in guest mode without an account. When you sign in with Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, we collect:

  • Email address
  • User name
  • Unique user ID provided by the authentication provider (Google or Apple)
  • Profile photo (if available)

Your name, email address, user ID and sign-in provider are kept in a user profile in our Firebase database (Cloud Firestore).

Note: When using Sign in with Apple, you have the option to hide your email address. In that case, Apple provides a private relay email address.

2.2 Usage and Analytics Data

Through Firebase Analytics, we collect:

  • Authentication events (login, logout, account deletion) — including email address and user ID
  • Content interaction events: multitrack names, track names, and playlist names
  • Playback settings: pitch transposition values, track volume levels, loop state
  • Export activity: file format and duration of exported audio
  • Audio routing changes (output bus index)
  • Subscription and paywall events (which screen was shown, purchases started or completed)
  • Screen views and general navigation patterns
  • Device information (model, operating system, app version) and an app instance identifier
  • Approximate location (country and city, derived automatically by Firebase from device locale and IP address)

2.3 Crash and Diagnostic Data

Through Firebase Crashlytics, when the application crashes or hits a serious error we collect the crash report (stack trace and the app's recent internal events), device model, operating system, app version, available memory and disk space, and an installation identifier. When you are signed in, the report includes your user ID so we can answer you about a problem you reported.

2.4 Multitrack Projects, Audio Files and Recordings

  • Multitracks (projects), tracks, and audio files you import are stored locally on your device
  • Supported audio formats: MP3, WAV, AIFF, M4A, AAC, FLAC and the other formats your device supports
  • The application uses the microphone only when you start a recording. Recordings are saved on your device and are never uploaded
  • Each user has their own separate and secure multitrack data - each user can only see and access their own multitracks and files
  • We do not upload, share, or have access to your audio files or recordings
  • Track configuration data (volume, pan, mute, order) is stored in the application's local database

2.5 Locally Stored Data

  • Track configurations (volume, pan, mute, order)
  • Multitrack, playlist and project names
  • User session information
  • User preferences (auto-pan, auto-play delay, waveform zoom, initial volume, auto-color)
  • App appearance preference (dark, light, or system mode)
  • Unique user ID (linked to authentication provider)

2.6 Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The free version of the application displays ads through Google AdMob.

  • On iOS: depending on your choice in the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog, AdMob may use your Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to show personalized ads. If you deny tracking, AdMob shows non-personalized ads without using the IDFA or tracking your cross-app activity.
  • On Android: AdMob uses the Android Advertising ID to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).
  • AdMob also receives device information (model, operating system, language), IP address and ad interaction data (impressions and taps).

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, advertising consent is managed through the Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) SDK, which presents GDPR-compliant consent forms. You can change your choice at any time from the application's settings.

2.7 Subscriptions (PRO)

PRO subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive or store your card or payment details. To know which features you have unlocked, we use Adapty, which receives your purchase records (product, dates, price, currency, trial and renewal status), an anonymous Adapty profile ID, your user ID when you are signed in, and device information (model, operating system, language, country derived from your IP address).

2.8 Talk to the Developer

If you are signed in, you can write to the developer from the application. We store the conversations you start (title, category, your messages and any images you choose to attach) in our Firebase database, linked to your user ID, and use them only to answer you and improve the application. Images are picked by you through the system photo picker; the application does not access your photo library otherwise.

To let you know when the developer replies, the application uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, which assigns your device a notification token. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

2.9 In-App Web Browser

The chord browser feature includes an in-app web browser that can load any external website. Regarding this browser:

  • The application does not record, log, or transmit the URLs you visit
  • External websites you visit have their own privacy policies and may use cookies, trackers, or other data collection technologies independent of this application
  • When you choose to import chord content, the application sends a network request to the currently viewed website to extract that content — no personal data or credentials are included in these requests
  • You are responsible for reviewing the privacy policies of external websites you choose to visit

2.10 Tutorial Videos

The application includes a tutorial section that embeds videos via a YouTube player. YouTube is operated by Google LLC and may collect data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Authentication: Verify your identity and manage your session
  • Functionality: Provide application features (audio playback, track management, pitch transposition, recording)
  • Subscriptions: Know which PRO features you have unlocked and restore your purchases
  • Advertising: Display ads through Google AdMob in the free version, personalized only where you allow it
  • Export: Process your multitrack project locally on your device to generate an audio file in the format you choose
  • Support: Answer the messages you send through Talk to the Developer
  • Service Improvement: Analyze application usage to improve user experience
  • Troubleshooting: Identify and resolve crashes and technical issues
  • Communication: Notify you of replies and important information about the application

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Local Storage

  • Application data is stored locally on your device, in the application's private storage
  • Audio files and recordings remain on your device and are not synced with external servers
  • Exported audio files are generated entirely on your device and are only shared externally when you explicitly choose to do so through the system share menu

5.2 Data Stored on Our Servers

Only your user profile and your conversations with the developer are stored on our servers (Google Firebase).

5.3 Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:

  • All data sent over the network is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Each user can only read their own profile and conversations
  • Authentication through trusted services (Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple)

However, no method of Internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

  • Session data: Retained while your session is active or until you log out
  • Local data: Retained on your device until you uninstall the application, delete your account or manually delete the data
  • User profile: Retained until you delete your account
  • Conversations with the developer: Retained until you ask us to delete them or delete your account as described in Section 7.2
  • Crash reports: Kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days
  • Analytics data: Managed by Firebase according to their retention policies
  • Purchase records: Kept by Adapty, Apple and Google as long as needed for billing, accounting and legal obligations

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we have about you
  • Rectify: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Revoke consent: Change your advertising consent, log out and stop using the application at any time

To exercise these rights, you can:

  • Log out from the application
  • Delete your account through the Account screen in the app (see Section 7.1)
  • Request deletion without the app (see Section 7.2)
  • Uninstall the application to delete all local data
  • Contact us at the address provided in Section 11

7.1 Account Deletion in the App

You can delete your account directly from within the application:

  • How: Go to Account → Delete Account and confirm (you may be asked to sign in again)
  • What is deleted immediately:
    • Your account and authentication credentials
    • Your user profile on our servers
    • All multitracks, tracks, recordings and audio files associated with your account on the device
    • Your preferences and session information
  • Confirmation: You will see a confirmation message once deletion is complete

Note: This action cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

7.2 Deletion Without the App

If you no longer have the application, or you also want your conversations with the developer deleted, follow the steps on our account deletion page. We delete your account, your profile and your conversations within 30 days, and confirm it by email. Analytics and crash data are not linked to your account once it is deleted and expire under the periods in Section 6; purchase records are kept only as long as the law requires.

8. Children's Privacy

The Multitrack Player is intended for users 13 years of age and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected information from a minor under 13, we will delete it immediately.

9. International Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, where data protection laws may differ. By using the application, you consent to these transfers.
