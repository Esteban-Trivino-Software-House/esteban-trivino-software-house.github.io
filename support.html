Frequently asked questions

How do I import audio?

In the app, choose the option to add or import tracks and select audio files from the Files picker. Imported audio is added to your project.

Which audio formats can I use?

The app supports MP3, WAV, AIFF, M4A, AAC, ALAC, CAF and other audio formats supported by iOS. Compatibility can depend on the encoding of a file.

Where are my projects and audio stored?

Imported multitrack projects and audio files are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded by the app. Exported files leave the device only when you choose to share them using iOS sharing options.

Can I use the app without an internet connection?

Playback and local project work are available offline after you have signed in. Online chord browsing and account-related services need a connection. Stem separation can be used offline after its model has been downloaded.

How do I sign in?

You can sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple. Follow the prompts shown by the provider you choose.

How can I delete my account?

Open the Account screen in the app and choose Delete Account. This permanently deletes the account and associated app data as described in the Privacy Policy.

Which devices are supported?

The app is for iPhone and iPad and requires iOS 17 or later. Check your device's software version in Settings.

Report a problem or share an idea

When contacting support, include what you were trying to do, the steps that led to the issue, your device model, iOS version and app version. A screenshot can help when relevant.

Send feature suggestions or bug reports to multitrack-player-app@outlook.com.
